## Med-CORDEX phase 3: Ocean stand-alone modelling

As the only coordinated initiative delivering ocean variables from regional climate models to the IPCC-AR6 interactive atlas, 
Med-CORDEX has traditionally fostered collaboration among ocean model developers and users since its inception. 
Now entering a new era centered on fully coupled climate modelling, the initiative has launched an Ocean Stand-alone Modelling activity 
to provide a controlled framework for evaluating, understanding, and improving ocean model performance, 
as well as assessing the contribution of ocean-driven processes to regional climate signals. 
Building on the legacy of the Ocean Model Intercomparison Project (OMIP; Griffies et al. 2016), 
this activity complements ongoing Med-CORDEX efforts through five main objectives: 

- establish standardized protocols for systematic model inter-comparison and assess the robustness of key physical mechanisms;
- identify the origins and consequences of shared model biases and uncertainties;
- test new parameterizations and provide a foundation for improving model physics;
- evaluate the benefits and challenges of emerging machine-learning and hybrid modelling approaches;
- broaden engagement with research groups studying ocean processes and strengthen community-wide collaboration. 

This activity will also serve as a hub for communication among modelling groups, promoting the exchange of technical implementations and best practices, 
including model tuning and spinup strategies and approaches for coupling with other Earth-system components.
