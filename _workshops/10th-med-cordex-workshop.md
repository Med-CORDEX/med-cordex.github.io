The 10th Med-CORDEX workshop will be organised in Palma de Mallorca, Spain

The main goal of the workshop is to further foster scientific exchanges among the Mediterranean regional climate modelling community and
to configure the near-future of Med-CORDEX in the global context of climate research. The workshop objectives are:

- To have scientific talks and exchanges to raise momentum in the community
- To organize the Med-CORDEX contribution to IPCC AR7
- To get an updated status of the CORDEX-CMIP6 Baseline runs and related scientific analyses
- To get updates from the new initiatives within the network (e.g. ocean biogeochemistry, coastal risk, seasonal-to-decadal, ocean stand-alone modelling, statistical downscaling)
- To enlarge the community: new modelling groups, model output users
- To improve the sharing and usability of the Med-CORDEX dataset (ESGF publication, model documentation)
